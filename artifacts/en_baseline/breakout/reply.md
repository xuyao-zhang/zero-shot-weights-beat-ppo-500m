### Design Overview

#### 1. Strategy
The policy combines **exact physics-based trajectory prediction** (maximizing survival under 10% sticky actions) with **exact brick-lookahead aiming at impact** (maximizing clearing speed while provably preventing infinite loops):

1. **Exact Velocity Recovery**: From the one-hot `ball` (ch 3) and `trail` (ch 4) channels, the network recovers the ball's horizontal sign `dx` (`±1`) and vertical sign `down` (`±1`), including all edge cases where `ball_x == trail_x` (wall/corner bounces and reset: `x = 0` always implies `dx = +1`, `x = 9` always implies `dx = -1`) and `ball_y == trail_y` (`y <= 4` implies `down = +1`, `y = 8` implies `down = -1`).
2. **Exact Landing Prediction & Sticky-Action Cushioning (`y <= 7`)**:
   - Because the ball position is one-hot encoded in the 100 inputs of channel 3, `W1` directly stores exact precomputed lookup tables `target_d(y, x)` for all 4 directions `d ∈ {UL, UR, DR, DL}`, accounting for the game's 20-state wall-clamp reflection (`0..9, 9..0`).
   - While moving **down** (`y <= 7`), `target` is the exact midpoint of the two-cell catch zone `{ball_x_at_8, new_x_at_9}` (or `-0.5` / `9.5` for the single-cell wall-corner catches `{0}` and `{9}`). Using a deadzone of `0.75` makes the paddle stop as soon as it enters `{L, R}`, leaving the full 2-cell cushion to absorb sticky-action overshoots (`10×`–`100×` lower death rate than chasing a single side early).
   - While moving **up**, `target` pre-positions immediately at the lowest-possible bounce landing (row 3 for `y = 4..8`, then smoothly updating to row 2 at `y = 3`, row 1 at `y = 2`, and ceiling at `y = 1, 0`), ensuring the paddle arrives several steps early even for corner-to-corner bounces.
3. **Zero-Risk Aiming & Loop-Free Brick Clearing (`y == 8`)**:
   - At the impact row `y == 8` (moving down), nudging from one cell of `{L, R}` to the other has **identical** survival probability under sticky actions to holding still (`no-op`), for every possible previous action.
   - By default, `target(8, x) = x` (`ball_x`), which preserves horizontal direction (`no reversal`). On empty trips this shifts the 20-state horizontal phase by `-2 (mod 20)` every bounce, cycling through all 10 phases of that parity and visiting every single brick cell in rows `1..3` twice—**mathematically guaranteeing zero infinite loops**.
   - Additionally, for all 18 non-corner impact states `(x_8, DR/DL)`, exact brick-lookahead units check whether the default launch trajectory `s_same` hits zero bricks while the reversed launch trajectory `s_rev` (`pos = new_x`) hits at least one brick. If so—and only when the paddle is already in `{L, R}`—the network overrides the impact-step nudge to catch at `new_x` and break a brick immediately instead of wasting an empty trip.

#### 2. Hidden Unit Layout
- **Layer 1 (`h1`, 32/32 units)**:
  - `h1[0]`: `dx ∈ {-1, +1}` (ball horizontal direction).
  - `h1[1]`: `down ∈ {-1, +1}` (ball vertical direction).
  - `h1[2..9]`: `want_R_d`, `want_L_d ∈ {-1, +1}` for `d = 0 (UL), 1 (UR), 2 (DR), 3 (DL)`, testing `target_d(ball) - pos > 0.75` and `pos - target_d(ball) > 0.75`.
  - `h1[10..18]`: `ov_DR[0..8]` — `+1` iff `ball == (8, x)` AND `s_same` has 0 bricks AND `s_rev` has ≥ 1 brick (for down-right impact).
  - `h1[19..27]`: `ov_DL[1..9]` — same for down-left impact.
  - `h1[28..31]`: `ge_m1, ge_0, ge_p1, ge_p2` — threshold comparisons of `pos - ball_x` (at row 8) against `-1.5, -0.5, +0.5, +1.5`, identifying exact paddle placement inside `{L, R}`.
- **Layer 2 (`h2`, 12/32 units used)**:
  - `h2[0..3]`: `go_R_d` (3-input AND of direction `d` and `want_R_d`).
  - `h2[4..7]`: `go_L_d` (3-input AND of direction `d` and `want_L_d`).
  - `h2[8..11]`: 4 impact-step override gates (`ov_DR` at `pos == ball_x` flips `no-op → right`; `ov_DR` at `pos == ball_x + 1` flips `left → no-op`; `ov_DL` at `pos == ball_x` flips `no-op → left`; `ov_DL` at `pos == ball_x - 1` flips `right → no-op`).
- **Output (`logits`, 6 actions)**: Sharp deterministic logits (`+20` chosen vs `0` no-op vs `-20` opposite; unused no-ops `u, d, f` set to `-40`).
